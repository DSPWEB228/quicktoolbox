Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This policy explains what information QuickToolBox ("we", "us") collects when you use this website, and how it is used. By using the site you agree to this policy.

Your files and text stay on your device

All tools on QuickToolBox — image compressor, PDF tools, QR code generator, text summarizer, word counter, converters and calculators — run in your browser. The images, PDFs, documents and text you select or type into a tool are processed on your own device and are not uploaded to our servers or stored by us.

Information we collect

We log minimal, anonymous usage events to understand how the site is used: the page that was viewed, which tool was used, whether an ad was shown or clicked, a timestamp, and a random identifier that exists only for the current browser tab session (kept in your browser's session storage and cleared when you close the tab). These events do not include your name, email address, or the contents of anything you type or upload. We do not store your IP address in these events.

If you email us, we receive your email address and the message you send, and we use them only to reply.

Cookies and advertising

This site may display ads served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's previous visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info. To learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services, see policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Where required by law (for example for visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), we use Google's consent messaging so you can choose whether to allow cookies and personalised ads.

Ads on this site are labelled "Advertisement". We may also show ads we arrange directly; these are labelled the same way.

Third-party services

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and some scripts from Google (Firebase) and public content delivery networks. Some tools load open-source libraries from these networks to work in your browser. These providers may receive your IP address and browser details as a normal part of serving those files. Site hosting and anonymous usage logs are provided by Google Firebase.

Children

This site is a general audience site and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, use the opt-out links above, or use a browser's privacy mode. The tools will keep working.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
