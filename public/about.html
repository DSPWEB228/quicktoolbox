About QuickToolBox

QuickToolBox is a small collection of free, everyday web tools — compress an image, merge photos into a PDF, count your words, work out a loan EMI, add GST to a price. Each one does a single job, loads quickly and works without an account or an install.

Why it exists

Many online tools ask you to upload your files to a stranger's server, sign up, or wade through pop-ups just to do a ten-second task. QuickToolBox is built the other way round: the tool is the first thing on the page, and wherever possible the work happens inside your own browser.

Your files stay on your device

Every tool on this site runs in your browser. The image compressor, PDF tools, QR generator, summarizer, converters and calculators process what you type or select locally on your device — your files and text are not uploaded to our servers. You can read the details in our privacy policy.

What you will find here

  • Image and PDF tools — image compressor, PDF compressor, JPG to PDF, and a PDF ⇄ Word text converter.
  • Text tools — word and character counter, text summarizer.
  • Generators and converters — QR code generator and a unit converter for length, weight and temperature.
  • Calculators — percentage, age, BMI, loan EMI and Indian GST calculators.

Each tool page explains how to use it, what its limits are, and answers common questions, so you know what to expect before you start.

How the site is funded

QuickToolBox is free to use. The site may show advertising, including Google AdSense, to cover hosting and development time. Ads are always labelled and never replace the tool itself.

Accuracy

We test the tools and fix mistakes when we find them, but results are provided for general information. Calculators such as BMI, EMI and GST give estimates — please check important financial, tax or health decisions with a qualified professional or your lender.
