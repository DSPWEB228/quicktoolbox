Free tools for everyday tasks

QuickToolBox brings together the small utilities people look for every day: shrinking a photo before emailing it, turning pictures into one PDF, counting the words in an essay, generating a QR code for a link, or working out a loan EMI and GST amount. There is nothing to install and no account to create.

Private by design

Every tool runs inside your browser. The files and text you use stay on your device instead of being sent to a server, which keeps things fast and keeps your documents yours. Read more on the About page and in the privacy policy.

How to pick a tool

Use the Image and PDF tools to reduce file size or convert formats, the Text tools to count or summarize writing, the Converter and Generator tools for units and QR codes, and the Calculator tools for percentages, age, BMI, loan EMI and GST. Each tool page has short instructions, tips and answers to common questions.
