About this tool

The PDF ⇄ Word Converter pulls text out of a PDF into a Word document, or turns a Word document into a PDF. It runs in your browser and is meant for text content.

How to use it

  1. Choose the direction: PDF to Word, or Word to PDF.
  2. Select your file.
  3. Start the conversion.
  4. Download the result and review it.

Tips and limits

PDF to Word downloads a .doc file that Word opens; Word to PDF expects a .docx file. Only text and paragraph breaks carry over. Complex layouts, tables, images and exact fonts will not be preserved, so always review the converted file. For layout-critical documents, use the original software.

Frequently asked questions

Will the formatting be identical?

No. The tool rebuilds a plain document from the text, so complex formatting is lost.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Conversion happens on your device.

Does it work on scanned PDFs?

Scanned PDFs contain images rather than text, so there is no text to extract.
