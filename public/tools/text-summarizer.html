Text Summarizer

Paste text below and get a short summary. This runs entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Summary

About this tool

The Text Summarizer picks the most important sentences from a passage you paste in and returns a short summary. It uses a simple word-frequency method in your browser, not an AI model.

How to use it

  1. Paste the text you want summarized.
  2. Choose how many sentences the summary should contain.
  3. Click Summarize.
  4. Read the summary and copy what you need.

Tips and limits

This is an extractive summarizer: it selects existing sentences rather than rewriting them, so it works best on well-structured articles and reports. Always check the summary against the original for anything important.

Frequently asked questions

Is this AI?

No. It scores sentences by how often their words appear in the text.

Is my text uploaded?

No. It stays in your browser.

Why does the summary read oddly?

Because sentences are picked from the original and not rewritten, they can feel disconnected.
