About this tool

The GST Calculator adds GST to a pre-tax amount, or removes GST from a GST-inclusive amount, and shows the CGST and SGST split.

How to use it

  1. Choose Add GST or Remove GST.
  2. Enter the amount in rupees.
  3. Pick a GST rate, or enter a custom rate.
  4. Read the net amount, total GST, gross amount and CGST/SGST split.

Tips and limits

Since 22 September 2025 India's main GST slabs are 5% and 18%, with 40% for certain luxury and sin goods, and 0% for exempt items. The old 12% and 28% slabs no longer apply to most goods, though you can enter any rate using Custom. Always confirm the correct rate for your product or service with the official GST rate schedule or a tax professional.

Frequently asked questions

What are CGST and SGST?

For sales within a state, GST is split equally between the central (CGST) and state (SGST) governments. Sales between states use IGST instead.

How do I remove GST from a price?

Choose Remove GST and enter the GST-inclusive price.

Is this tax advice?

No, it is a calculator for estimates.
