About this tool

The Word & Character Counter shows words, characters (with and without spaces), sentences and estimated reading time while you type or paste text.

How to use it

  1. Type or paste your text into the box.
  2. Watch the counts update instantly.
  3. Edit your text until you reach the length you need.

Tips and limits

Reading time is estimated at roughly 200 words per minute, a common average for adults. Platforms count characters differently (some include spaces, some do not), so check both numbers against the limit you are working to.

Frequently asked questions

How are words counted?

Words are groups of characters separated by spaces or line breaks.

Is my text saved?

No. It is processed in your browser and not stored.

Is the reading time exact?

No, it is an estimate based on an average reading speed.
